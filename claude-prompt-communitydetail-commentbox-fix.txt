Context: bugfix on top of the just-applied Community Detail card redesign. The "Comments" section (`.second-box-config`) and its rounded comment bar (`.leave-comment`) never had an explicit `background` set, so the page's beige background (`#e8e0d5`, set globally in `scss/app.scss` — NOT touched here) shows through behind them, and the plain-rectangle `<input>` doesn't get visually clipped to the bar's rounded corners — producing the broken look in the screenshot (beige patches poking through at the rounded corners and around the button). Fix: give both containers the same white card background already used by the sibling `.first-box-config` card directly above it, and clip the input to the bar's rounded shape. Pure CSS, no JSX/logic touched.
