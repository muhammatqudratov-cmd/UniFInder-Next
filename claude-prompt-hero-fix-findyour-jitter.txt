Goal: Fix "Find your" text jitter in libs/components/layout/LayoutHome.tsx

Problem: hero-section has text-align:center. When the animated word changes length
(e.g. "campus" 6 chars → "university" 10 chars), the centered line shifts horizontally,
making "Find your" appear to jump left/right.

Fix: Add minWidth:'6em' + textAlign:'center' to the word span so the line width stays
constant regardless of which word is showing. Also make the slide animation gentler
(smaller translateY, ease-out easing).

STRICT RULES:
- Do NOT touch canvas animation, router.push, buttons, subtitle, className values.
- Only touch: the <style> keyframes block and the <h1> block.
