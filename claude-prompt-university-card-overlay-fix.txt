Context: presentational-only change to the university card used on `/agent/detail` (and everywhere else it's reused — `UniversityBigCard`). In the target design, the price tag sits at the TOP-left of the photo, there's a separate circular "favorite" heart toggle at the TOP-right of the photo, and the bottom info row only shows the eye/view count (no separate like-count number). Currently the price sits at the BOTTOM-left of the photo, there is no heart button on the photo, and the bottom row shows both an eye+view-count AND a heart+like-count.

No GraphQL/Apollo logic changes here: the existing `likeUniversityHandler(user, university?._id)` call and the existing `university?.meLiked?.[0]?.myFavorite` check are reused exactly as-is — only their position in the JSX moves (from the bottom row onto the photo), and the now-redundant like-count `<Typography>` next to it is removed since the design no longer shows a number there (the heart icon itself still visually reflects liked/not-liked state).
