Context: bug fix for the Community page hero (`/community`, from `claude-prompt-community-hero-landing.txt` + `claude-prompt-community-heading-linebreak-fix.txt`). The hero currently breaks visually: the eyebrow ("COMMUNITY"), the heading ("Voices on" / "campus"), and the browsing card are three separate flex children of `.community-hero-container`, which has `justify-content: space-between`. That spreads all three apart evenly across the row, so the heading ends up floating in the middle of the hero, detached from its eyebrow, instead of sitting directly under it on the left. Fix: group the eyebrow + heading into one left-side column, keep the browsing card as the only other flex item on the right. This is a markup/CSS-only structural fix — no logic/data change, no change to the text content itself.
