--- STEP 2: Fix layout shift — add minWidth to the animated span ---

The span currently has no minWidth, so it collapses/expands with each word,
causing the h1 to change width and the navbar pill to jump.
Fix: reserve space equal to the longest word ("university" = 10ch).

Find:
										<span
											key={wordIdx}
											style={{
												color: '#e8856a',
												display: 'inline-block',
												animation: `${phase === 'in' ? 'wordSlideIn' : 'wordSlideOut'} 0.5s ease-out forwards`,
											}}
										>
											{WORDS[wordIdx]}
										</span>

Replace with:
										<span
											key={wordIdx}
											style={{
												color: '#e8856a',
												display: 'inline-block',
												minWidth: '10ch',
												textAlign: 'left',
												animation: `${phase === 'in' ? 'wordSlideIn' : 'wordSlideOut'} 0.5s ease-out forwards`,
											}}
										>
											{WORDS[wordIdx]}
										</span>
