Context: convert the desktop card grids in TopUniversities and PopularUniversities from a static grid into an auto-sliding Swiper carousel that:
- Shows exactly 4 cards at a time
- Automatically slides left every 2 seconds
- Loops infinitely (when the last card passes, the first reappears seamlessly)
- Has a smooth 600ms slide transition

Swiper, SwiperSlide, and Autoplay are ALREADY imported in both files (confirmed in the existing import section) — no new imports are needed.

No card content, no business logic, no Apollo queries, no click handlers, no like logic, and no prop types are changed. Only the outer container (`top-cards-grid` / `popular-cards-grid` div) is replaced with a Swiper, and each card is wrapped in a SwiperSlide.
